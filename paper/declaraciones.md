## 6. Artificial Intelligence Statement

Claude (Anthropic) was used throughout this work under the direct supervision and
review of the authors: to write and debug the data-processing, training,
statistical-analysis, and figure-generation code described in the Method section;
to verify the bibliographic references against their original sources; and to
draft and revise manuscript text from the authors' experimental design and
results. All code was executed and all reported numbers were checked by the
authors, who take full responsibility for the content of this article.
